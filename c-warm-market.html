Kids Eat Free

Kids 12 and under eat free with every adult entree when you dine in. They get a mini rice bowl with their choice of protein.

See the details
  • Kids 12 and under eat free with every adult entree. Children must be present at time of order.
  • An entree is a Premium Bowl, Sandweech, or Large Salad.
  • Kids get a mini rice bowl with limited toppings and a protein choice: White meat chicken, Gyro, Dark meat chicken, or Falafel.
  • Limit 1 free kids meal per child, maximum 2 per adult entree.
  • Dine-in only. One time per visit, single transaction. Not valid online or through third-party apps.
